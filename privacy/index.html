Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 20, 2026

PlayItLive Now is run by Xexex Enterprises LLC. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we keep

  • Account: your email address, and the date you confirmed your age and accepted the terms.
  • Date of birth: we check it when you join and don't save it.
  • Your page: your name, handle, location, bio, links and photos. These are public.
  • What you post: songs, lyrics, photos, videos, reels, posts, comments, Guestbook messages, room messages, ratings and shop listings. These are public, and so are your favorites and playlists.
  • Private messages: stored so you and the other person can read them. They aren't public. Staff can see one if it's reported, and may need to access stored messages to keep the site safe or if the law requires it.
  • Activity: follows, friends, likes, plays, tokens, poll votes, blocks, reports, suggestions you send us and notifications.
  • Payments: if you buy a membership or tokens, your Stripe customer number, membership status, purchase history and billing address. When sales tax applies, we use your billing address to work it out.

What we do with it

We use it only to run the site, keep it safe and answer you. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

When someone performs on The Open Stage, their voice passes through Cloudflare, our live audio provider, to reach listeners. We don't record it, but anyone listening could.

Who helps us run the site

  • Supabase stores accounts, data and files, in the United States.
  • Resend sends our emails, such as sign-in links, so it receives your email address.
  • Stripe handles payments. If you buy something, Stripe receives your email, card and billing address. We never see your full card number, and we keep your Stripe customer number, membership status and purchase history.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code to your browser, so they see your IP address.
  • Suno streams the audio of songs added by a Suno link, so it sees the listener's IP address. Suno's own privacy policy applies.
  • YouTube loads when you watch an embedded video, and we ask it for a video's title when you add a YouTube link without one. Google's own privacy policy applies.

Merch Booth purchases happen on the artist's own payment page, under that service's privacy policy.

On your device

Your browser stores your sign-in session and a few settings, such as muted genres and reduced motion. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Tracking and "Do Not Track"

We don't track you across other websites, and we don't let advertisers or analytics companies do so on our site. The services listed above receive your IP address and basic device information only to deliver their part of the page, and their own policies cover what they do with it. Because we don't do cross-site tracking, we treat every visitor the same whether or not their browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal.

Emails

We email you about your account, such as sign-in links and important notices about your membership or these policies. We don't send marketing emails unless you've chosen to get them, and you can stop them anytime.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove it as described below. We keep payment and purchase records for as long as tax and accounting laws require, even after an account is deleted.

Keeping it safe

We use security rules that limit who can read and change each kind of data, encrypted connections, and providers who protect what they store. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise nothing will ever go wrong. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Deleting your information

Delete your account any time from My studio. That removes your profile, songs, posts, messages you sent and uploaded files. If you joined through someone's invite link, we keep a scrambled code made from your email address, so the same address can't earn a second invite reward. If you made a report, or someone reported you, we may keep that record to keep the site safe. Copies can stay in backups for a short time. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your information at support@playitlivenow.com.

Adults only

The site is for people 18 and older, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a child has an account, please report it or email us, and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell members on the site or by email before it applies.
